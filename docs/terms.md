Driftlog is a personal aquarium log. It records tanks, livestock, equipment, parameter readings, journal entries, and reminders. It is a tool for hobbyists; it is not a veterinary product or a water-chemistry diagnostic device.

## Use the results carefully

Driftlog is provided as-is, for personal information purposes only. **Do not rely on parameter ranges, reminders, or any other output of the app as veterinary, medical, or chemical advice.** Suitable parameter ranges vary by species, by region, and by individual setup. Always confirm against authoritative sources (qualified aquarium veterinarians, peer-reviewed species references, manufacturer documentation) before making decisions that affect the health of your animals.
