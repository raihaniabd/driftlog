What it does

A focused tool for serious hobbyists — no clutter, no upsells.

🐟

Living Tank

Your fish, swimming. A live, animated view of every tank you keep, with parameter readouts overlaid. Tap a fish for its age, species, and history.

📈

Parameters & charts

Log pH, ammonia, nitrate, salinity, alkalinity, calcium, magnesium — whatever your tank type needs. See trends over 7, 30, 90 days, or all time.

⏰

Reminders that actually wake you

Water changes, tests, dosing. Time-Sensitive iOS notifications break through Focus Mode and Do Not Disturb — so you never forget a maintenance window.

🪸

Livestock & equipment

Auto-calculated ages, a memorial section for fish that have passed, photos, notes, and tracked install/service intervals for every piece of gear.

📓

Journal

Free-text dated entries with optional photos. Status changes auto-write entries too, so your journal becomes a real chronicle of the tank.

🌍

Five languages

English, العربية (with full RTL), Français, Español, Deutsch — pick your language in Settings.

Private by design

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. No ads.

⛔

Zero network calls

Driftlog has no backend. Everything you log — tanks, fish, photos, parameters — is written to your device's local database. The app makes no requests in normal use.

🔒

Your data stays local

Tank data lives in the iOS app sandbox on your device. We have no copy. We could not retrieve it on your behalf if you asked.

↩︎

Export anytime

Settings → Data → Export parameters (CSV) hands your readings to the iOS share sheet. Send them to Files, AirDrop, email, anywhere.
